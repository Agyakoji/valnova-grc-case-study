# ValNova Risk Register

## Overview

This risk register was developed as part of the ValNova Technologies GRC case study.

ValNova is a fictional Nigerian fintech company operating across payment, identity verification, and government-facing digital services. The exercise was designed to apply risk management concepts in a practical organizational context.

## Purpose

The purpose of this risk register is to:

- Identify risks that could affect ValNova's business objectives
- Assess likelihood and potential impact
- Assign risk ownership
- Document proposed risk treatments
- Assess expected residual risk
- Support risk-based decision-making
- Provide a structured basis for ongoing monitoring and review

## Risk Assessment Approach

Each risk is assessed based on its likelihood and potential impact before treatment.

Proposed treatments are then considered to determine the expected residual exposure after the controls or actions are implemented.

The register is intended to be reviewed and updated as the organization's objectives, operating environment, controls, and risk landscape change.

## What This Demonstrates

This artefact demonstrates practical application of:

- Risk identification
- Risk analysis
- Risk evaluation
- Risk treatment
- Risk ownership
- Residual risk assessment
- Risk monitoring

## Important Note

ValNova Technologies is a fictional company created for this GRC portfolio project.

The risk scenarios, assessments, and proposed treatments are part of the case study and should not be interpreted as an assessment of a real organization.
