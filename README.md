# ValNova GRC Case Study

## Overview

This repository contains a Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) technology evaluation conducted for a fictional fintech company, **ValNova Technologies**.

The objective was to assess leading GRC platforms and recommend the most suitable solution based on business needs, regulatory requirements, scalability, implementation complexity, and total cost of ownership.

---

## Business Scenario

ValNova is a fictional fintech experiencing rapid growth and expanding into multiple markets.

As regulatory obligations increase, the company requires a centralized GRC platform capable of supporting:

- Governance
- Enterprise Risk Management
- Compliance
- Audit Management
- Privacy
- Third-Party Risk

---

## Tools Evaluated

- Sprinto
- OneTrust
- Vanta
- Drata
- Hyperproof

---

## Evaluation Criteria

The platforms were evaluated using a weighted decision model based on:

- Framework Coverage
- Automation
- Vendor Risk Management
- Privacy Capabilities
- Reporting
- Scalability
- Ease of Implementation
- Cost

## Key Learning

This project reinforced an important lesson:

> Selecting a GRC platform is not about choosing the tool with the most features.

> It is about selecting the platform that best aligns with an organization's risk profile, regulatory obligations, operational maturity, and strategic goals.
